Plain.

A theme that lays a store out like a printed catalogue: a fixed navigation rail, numbered index entries, framed plates with figure captions, and a set of editorial sections to explain what you sell.

01

Overview

Plain is built on three ideas that repeat on every page.

  • The rail. On desktop the menu is a vertical rail fixed to the start edge of the page. Items are numbered, and items with sub-links slide a panel out beside the rail. Below 990 px the rail becomes a slim top bar with a full-screen Index menu.
  • Index entries. Products are listed as numbered entries: a catalogue number, the name, an optional key ingredient and concentration, the type and number of sizes, the price and a quick-add button. The same entry renders as a ruled list row or as a plate in a grid.
  • Plates. Every image sits in a plate: a paper matte, a keyline and a numbered caption (“Fig. 3”).

The theme is written for shops that need to explain their products, such as skincare, haircare, food, supplements and books, but nothing in it is tied to a category. Product details such as key ingredient are read from optional metafields (see Product page) and are skipped when empty.

02

Install

  1. In Shopify admin go to Online Store → Themes, add the theme and click Customize.
  2. Under Theme settings → Logo and monogram upload a logo, or type a two or three letter monogram. With neither, the initials of your store name are used.
  3. In the Navigation rail section (header group) choose your menu. It uses main-menu by default.
  4. Open the home page and click each section to choose your own products, collections and images. Empty slots show placeholders until you do.
  5. For product cards to show a key ingredient and concentration, fill the product metafields described in Product page. This is optional.
  6. Check the mobile view with the device toggle in the editor, then save.

The theme ships with starter text on the home page, About, Contact and FAQ templates. Replace it with your own; nothing in it is tied to a product or collection.

04

Header and navigation

The header group holds an announcement bar (Notice line) and the Navigation rail.

Desktop rail (990 px and wider)

The rail shows the logo or monogram, the numbered menu, search, account and cart. Items with sub-links open a panel beside the rail on hover, focus or click; the panel lists second-level links numbered like “2.1”, with third-level links beneath, and a link to the whole section. Keyboard: the arrow keys move between links and Esc closes the panel and returns focus to its item.

Mobile and tablet

A slim top bar shows the brand, search, cart and an Index button that opens a full-screen list of the same menu, numbered, with sub-links underneath.

Notice line

Add up to five Message blocks, each with text and an optional link, and choose the background and text colours. Messages sit side by side in one row; there is no rotation.

05

Index entries and plates

Wherever the theme lists products (home, collection, search, recommendations) it uses the same entry. Sections that list products let you choose List or Plates as the default. The home index, collection pages and search results add a toggle so visitors can switch; collection pages remember the choice for the visit.

  • List. A ruled row per product. On devices with a mouse, hovering or focusing a row lifts its plate beside the row. On touch devices and below 990 px each row shows a small plate at its start.
  • Plates. A grid of framed plates with the number, name, details and price under each. Columns are set per section (2–5 on desktop).

Each entry shows the catalogue number (position in the list), name, key ingredient and concentration when set, product type and number of sizes, price (with “From” for variable prices) and a quick-add button. Products with a single variant add straight to the cart; products with options open a quick view with the variant picker.

Marginal notes

Instead of coloured badges, small labels are printed beside the entry number: Sold out, Reduced (as a percentage or as a word), New entry and Scarce. The last two are driven by product tags, which you name in Theme settings → Index entries (defaults new and selling-fast).

08

Collection and search

Collection pages (Catalogue index) show a breadcrumb, entry count, title and description, then a toolbar and the entries.

  • Filtering. The Catalogue drawer button opens a drawer with your Search & Discovery filters (price range, availability, and any product filters you configure). Results update in place without reloading, active filters appear as removable chips, and the URL stays shareable.
  • Sorting. The sort menu lists the collection’s sort options. Sorting A–Z or Z–A adds letter dividers between groups of entries.
  • Presentation. List or plates, with a visitor toggle.
  • Pagination. Entries per page (8–48), with page links.
  • Empty results. A “No products found” message with a clear-all button.

The search page uses the same entries, with filtering, and lists matching pages and articles too. The search drawer offers suggestions (queries, collections, pages, articles and products with price) while typing; turn suggestions and prices off in Theme settings → Search.

The collection list page (Table of chapters) numbers collections with Roman numerals, with entry counts. Choose collections and sort order, and hide empty ones.

09

Cart

Choose between a drawer and a page in Theme settings → Cart. The drawer opens after any add to cart; the page (Order sheet) is a numbered list of lines with small plates.

  • Quantity buttons and remove, updated without reloading.
  • Free shipping progress. Set a threshold in your store currency (0 hides it).
  • Gift wrap. Pick a product in Theme settings → Cart; a checkbox on the cart page adds or removes it.
  • Order note and a shipping estimator (country, province, postal code) on the cart page; each can be turned off.
  • Accelerated checkout buttons, and cross-references (recommendations) under the cart.

12

Languages and RTL

Storefront text lives in locales/en.default.json and editor labels in en.default.schema.json. Translate them with the Translate & Adapt app or by adding locale files. Text you type in the editor is translated in the same app.

The footer can show country/currency and language selectors when your store has more than one of each (turn them off in the Colophon section). For Arabic, Hebrew, Persian and Urdu the page direction is set to right-to-left automatically. The stylesheet uses logical properties throughout, so the rail, panels, drawers, arrows, entries and tables mirror without a separate stylesheet. User-entered text is set with automatic direction so mixed-language content reads correctly.

13

Performance and accessibility

  • A shared stylesheet plus small per-section styles, and one deferred script; no third-party scripts or libraries. Fonts come from Shopify’s font CDN and the body and display fonts are preloaded.
  • Images at the top of a page (the masthead plate, the product image, the first entries of a list) load eagerly with high fetch priority; the rest are lazy and use responsive srcset. Reveal-on-scroll only hides content that starts below the fold, so it never delays the first paint.
  • Every page has one h1 and an unbroken heading order, a skip link, labelled form fields, visible focus outlines and descriptive names for icon buttons.
  • Drawers, the quick view and the lightbox keep keyboard focus inside while open, close with Esc and return focus to the control that opened them. The rail panels are keyboard operable.
  • Default colours meet WCAG AA contrast (4.5:1 for text). If you change colours, the editor notes give the ratio to keep.
  • Links, filter forms and the add-to-cart form work without JavaScript; in-place filtering, cart updates and quick view are enhancements.

14

Support

Product cards show no key ingredient.

Create the sixth.key_ingredient and sixth.concentration metafield definitions with storefront access, fill them on the product, and check that Show key ingredient and concentration is on in Theme settings → Index entries.

The formula number is missing on the product page.

The number is the product’s position in the Catalogue collection chosen in the product section. Choose a collection that contains the product; only the first 50 products are counted.

The rail shows the wrong menu.

Open the Navigation rail section in the header group and select the menu. Sub-links appear as panels only for items that have child links.

Gift wrap does nothing.

Choose the gift wrap product in Theme settings → Cart. The product must be published to the Online Store.

For help with the theme, contact the theme author through the support link in your Shopify theme library.
